About the platform

Helping remote workers recognize and defend against social engineering attacks, one module at a time.

Our mission

Remote workers and virtual assistants are increasingly targeted by phishing, impersonation, and other social engineering attacks, often without the in-house security training available to office-based employees. This platform was built to close that gap with free, accessible foundational training, and specialized role-based modules for those working directly with international clients.
